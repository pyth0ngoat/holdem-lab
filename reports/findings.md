### Model M1 (Normalized Power Law)
$$\hat{E}_n(h) = \frac{1}{n} \cdot \frac{(2 \cdot p_2(h))^{\beta_n}}{\kappa_n}$$

- Given $p_2(\text{KK}) = 0.8288$.

- Power: $1.6576^{1.460} \approx 2.0914$.

## 10. Open Limitations
1. **Showdown selection bias in PHH data:** Hole cards are only observed for hands reaching showdown; folded hands remain unobserved, inflating observed showdown hand quality.
2. **Equal-contribution / Limp-or-fold abstraction:** Does not reflect no-limit bet sizing, aggressive bluffing, or fold equity.
3. **Board card correlation:** iid order statistics assume independent draws, ignoring the 5 shared community cards which induce positive dependence.
4. **Range sensitivity:** Bot play rates prior to showdown may differ from showdown ranges.
5. **Continuous Beta model for discrete equity:** Beta distribution assumes continuous support on $[0, 1]$, whereas starting hands have discrete rank structures.
