## 7. Open Limitations
1. **Showdown selection bias in PHH data:** Hole cards are only observed for hands reaching showdown; folded hands remain unobserved.
2. **Equal-contribution / Limp-or-fold abstraction:** Does not reflect no-limit bet sizing, aggressive bluffing, or fold equity.
3. **Board card correlation:** iid order statistics assume independent draws, ignoring the 5 shared community cards.
4. **Range sensitivity:** Bot play rates prior to showdown may differ from showdown ranges.
