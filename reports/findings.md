## 6. Scope Map
| Aspect of poker | Status | What we do | Future study |
|---|---|---|---|
| Probability of hands and outcomes (combinatorics, equity) | **IN SCOPE (core)** | Exact counts, Monte Carlo and exact equity, equity tables, a simple formula | Omaha, other variants |
| Number of players (table size) | **IN SCOPE (core)** | Order statistics of the winning hand, equity vs n, rank changes across n | Position and seat effects |
| Imperfect information (unknown opponent cards) | **PARTLY** | Opponent cards modelled as uniformly random or as the top q% of hands (range model); selection bias measured | Bayesian updating of opponent ranges from actions; opponent modelling |
| Probability estimation by humans | **IN SCOPE** | Live demo: unaided vs aided guesses, calibration | Training effects over many sessions |
| Variance, luck and sample size | **IN SCOPE** | CLT convergence, sample size to separate strategies, ruin approximation, waiting times | Bankroll management policies |
| Long-term planning (blinds, many-hand results) | **PARTLY** | Blind cost model, long-run profit per hand, table composition effects | Dynamic programming of stack and blind schedules; tournaments and ICM |
| Simple strategies | **PARTLY** | Play-or-fold (limp-or-fold) strategies and range-width archetypes at different table sizes | Raising, bet sizing, bluffing, value betting, slow-play, fold equity |
| Multi-street decision making | **OFF LIMITS (analysis only)** | Equity over streets (martingale, variance by street) as pure probability; no betting policy | Postflop policies, reinforcement learning, counterfactual regret minimization |
| Game-theoretic equilibrium (GTO) | **OFF LIMITS** | Cited only (CFR, Libratus, Pluribus) | Solving small games; exploitability |
| Psychology (tilt, tells), real-money behaviour | **OFF LIMITS** | None | Behavioural studies |
| Real-money gambling advice | **OFF LIMITS** | Explicitly excluded | n/a |

## 7. Open Limitations
1. **Showdown selection bias in PHH data:** Hole cards are only observed for hands reaching showdown; folded hands remain unobserved.
2. **Equal-contribution / Limp-or-fold abstraction:** Does not reflect no-limit bet sizing, aggressive bluffing, or fold equity.
3. **Board card correlation:** iid order statistics assume independent draws, ignoring the 5 shared community cards.
4. **Range sensitivity:** Bot play rates prior to showdown may differ from showdown ranges.
